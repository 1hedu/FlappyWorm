Flappy Worm

The C. elegans wiring diagram learns to play Flappy Bird. The centre of each gap gives off a food smell. When the worm drifts away from the gap's height, the smell fades, its odour neuron AWC fires, and through the real wiring its escape neuron AVA rises; each fresh rise turns it back: a flap if it's sinking, a dive if it's climbing. Neural noise alone never triggers a turn-back. The worm starts with a modest innate attraction to the smell; every gap it passes through near the centre counts as a meal and strengthens AWC's synapses. Classic Flappy physics; behind the scenes the game clock runs 2.5× slower than the worm's nervous system, because a worm's reflex takes about half a second.

What's real here?

Real: the 302-neuron wiring (Cook et al. 2019), transmitter identities (Wang et al. 2024), the odour neuron AWC and its output synapses, and the turn-back reflex through AVA.

Modelled on real biology: the learning. Worms strengthen attraction to a cue that comes with food; here passing near a gap's centre counts as eating and scales AWC's output synapses up. Rates are compressed.

Engineered: the gap's smell (it depends on the worm's height relative to the gap centre and grows as the pipe approaches), reading a fresh rise of AVA above a threshold set from measured noise as "turn back" (flap if sinking, dive if climbing), gravity, pipes, slow motion. Learning off freezes the synapses; scrambled wiring shuffles synapse targets; the simple script reverses whenever the smell fades, with no neurons.

Checks: with neural noise switched off the worm flies just as well (smell drives it); with the smell switched off it never turns back and falls; stronger AWC synapses fly better (smell strength 0.4 about 6 of 24 pipes, 1.0 about 18); three degree-preserving shuffles of the wiring score 0; learning worms go from about 1 pipe in their first game to mostly full courses. Knocking out single neurons (32 games each): only AIB significantly hurts (9.6 vs 19.0 pipes), matching the published route by which AWC's "odour gone" signal drives reversals through AIB (Chalasani et al. 2007).

This is a toy model driven by a real wiring diagram, not an upload of a worm.
